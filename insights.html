Flagship themes

Most modernization problems are execution problems first

Projects do not usually fail because the tool category is mysterious. They fail because ownership, sequencing, communication, governance, and operational reality were underestimated before implementation began.

Automation without control is just faster chaos

Automation should make work more observable, repeatable, and governed. If the underlying process is chaotic, speed only helps the mess spread faster.

Blind trust is not an AI strategy

AI has a place in serious operations, but usefulness without exposure control becomes risk. Governance, fit assessment, policy, and human accountability matter.

The lens

I look at technology through the work it is supposed to improve: patient care, staff capability, decision quality, operational maturity, risk posture, and recoverability. If the system cannot be explained, governed, backed out, or lived with by the people doing the work, it is not finished.
